    zero-shot and three-shot evaluation. The RAG path was hardened so retrieved

    \QwenThirdRunRawOutputCount{} of 75 adapter outputs were raw, and many

    \QwenThirdRunTolerantValidity{}, relevance macro-F1 to
    \QwenThirdRunTolerantRelevanceMacroFOne{}, direction macro-F1 to
    \QwenThirdRunTolerantDirectionMacroFOne{}, symbol F1 to

    \texttt{marketgyan-qwen35-9b-targeted-v2} loaded. Direct unconstrained

    citations from retrieved rows.
    \item[July 3, 2026, proposal evaluation] The targeted-v2 model-only

    all-30-query zero-padded Precision@5 was 0.113. The system is therefore a
    grounded demo/research prototype, not a deployable market product.
