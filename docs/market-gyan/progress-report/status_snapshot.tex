  \begin{description}[style=nextline]
    \item[June 13, 2026] Data collection and API-assisted structuring reached
    78 valid candidates. Human review and approval remained the blocking
    condition for dataset export and model fine-tuning.
    \item[June 13, 2026, 22:55 NPT] Six-month collection completed with
    556 eligible documents. Structuring reached 204 valid candidates before
    sustained provider HTTP 500 responses paused the worker. The remaining 262
    queued jobs were resumable; the 450-candidate target and all human approval
    remained incomplete.
    \item[June 13, 2026, final collection audit] The Gemma worker resumed after
    a successful live API check and reached 452
    schema-valid, evidence-grounded candidates. This exceeds the
    \CandidateCollectionTarget{}-candidate collection target. Duplicate
    idempotency keys, invalid pending candidates, and over-length excerpts are
    all zero. Human approval remained at 0, so model
    training has not started.
    \item[June 14, 2026] Added two linear training notebooks, strict approval
    and class-coverage gates, a frozen split manifest, finance-specific Qdrant
    indexing and retrieval, a protected CrewAI service boundary, local
    OpenAI-compatible Qwen inference configuration, grounded report APIs,
    idempotent post-market orchestration, and system-evaluation tooling.
    These components are implemented but have not produced training or live
    retrieval results because all \PendingCandidateCount{} candidates still
    require human decisions. OCR and legacy-font conversion are explicitly
    excluded from the project.
    \item[June 15, 2026] Human review exposed a corpus-quality problem: several
    schema-v1 approvals were general business stories without a defensible
    NEPSE impact mechanism. The v1 corpus was frozen as a pilot audit with
    \PilotApprovedCount{} approved, \PilotRejectedCount{} rejected,
    \PendingCandidateCount{} pending, and \QueuedCandidateCount{} queued
    records. Schema v2, prompt \texttt{market-impact-v2}, a versioned Nepal
    market ontology, independent annotations, adjudication, sentence-ID
    evidence, targeted selection, market-reaction records, and revised model
    notebooks were implemented. No v2 candidates have yet been generated or
    adjudicated. A read-only selector found \VTwoDirectSelectionCount{} direct,
    \VTwoIndirectSelectionCount{} indirect, and
    \VTwoNegativeSelectionCount{} hard-negative candidates; therefore archive
    collection must continue into 2025 before the 350/100/100 corpus quota can
    be claimed.
    \item[June 15, 2026, 01:55 NPT] Targeted ShareSansar and resumable
    OnlineKhabar archive collection expanded the eligible pool to
    \EligibleDocumentCount{} documents. Exact-content deduplication removed
    \VTwoExactDuplicateCount{} duplicate candidates before selection. The
    resulting schema-v2 plan contains \VTwoDirectSelectionCount{} direct,
    \VTwoIndirectSelectionCount{} indirect, and
    \VTwoNegativeSelectionCount{} hard-negative records, with
    \VTwoEnglishSelectionCount{} English, \VTwoNepaliSelectionCount{} Nepali,
    \VTwoSymbolSelectionCount{} symbol-level records, at least 20 examples in
    every core event category, and no source above
    \VTwoLargestSourceCount{} records. All 500 valid candidates were queued, and a
    database-backed live Gemma smoke produced one valid pending candidate.
    Human annotation, adjudication, agreement measurement, reactions, and
    model experiments remain incomplete.
    \item[June 16, 2026, 12:15 NPT] The resumed Gemma worker increased
    schema-v2 pending-review records to 500 and left 0 queued, 0 processing,
    and 50 failed jobs. Of the failed jobs,
    \ValidationFailureCount{} are schema or evidence-validation failures and
    \RetryableFailureCount{} are retryable provider or response-format
    failures. Before later cleanup, Codex pre-review had covered 422 pending
    candidates, with 263 corrected suggestions and 159 confirmations. Human
    primary annotations stood at 200, but no schema-v2 record had been adjudicated
    into gold for training export.
    \item[June 16, 2026, 15:35 NPT] The candidate corpus target was reduced
    from 550 to \CandidateCollectionTarget{} adjudicated records. Four
    additional valid samples were recovered from retryable or stored Gemma
    outputs, bringing schema-v2 pending-review records to 500. The remaining
    \VTwoFailedCount{} records are retained
    as failed audit records and are outside the final candidate target.
    \item[June 16, 2026, 18:14 NPT] Unsubmitted Codex pre-review corrections
    were reverted at the database level. The 233 submitted primary annotations
    were preserved, including their assistant-review audit where present. The
    remaining unsubmitted queue now exposes raw Gemma candidates only, with
    zero assistant suggestions attached to unsubmitted pending-review records.
    \item[June 16, 2026, 20:17 NPT] All 500 submitted primary annotations were
    validated and batch-adjudicated into schema-v2 gold labels. A rebalance audit
    found the gold corpus at 252 direct, 95 indirect, and 153 not-relevant
    records, so the 300/100/100 relevance gate still fails. Event floors also
    remain short for earnings, credit/financing, regulation,
    monetary/liquidity, and sector/industry. Training export remains blocked
    until targeted replacements are reviewed and surplus hard negatives are
    excluded.
    \item[June 16, 2026, 20:40 NPT] Targeted rebalance completed. Fifty-three
    surplus not-relevant records were excluded, 48 direct and 5 indirect
    replacements were added, and 18 existing gold labels were reclassified to
    close event gaps. The final gold corpus contains 300 direct, 100 indirect,
    and 100 not-relevant records; 299 English, 200 Nepali, and 1 mixed-language
    record; 273 symbol-level records; and no source above 60 percent
    (ShareSansar 295, OnlineKhabar 201, Kathmandu Post 4). Event floors pass
    with earnings 36, credit/financing 20, regulation 20, monetary/liquidity
    20, and sector/industry 20. The approved JSONL was exported as
    \texttt{nepse-impact-500.jsonl}; validation, the Python dataset gate, and
    chronological duplicate-grouped train/validation/test splits of 350/75/75
    all passed.
    \item[June 17, 2026] The first baseline and Qwen training outputs were
    audited. XLM-R reached \XLMRRelevanceMacroFOne{} relevance macro-F1 and
    \XLMRDirectionMacroFOne{} direction macro-F1; English-only FinBERT reached
    \FinBERTMacroFOne{} macro-F1 on a small English relevant subset. Qwen
    QLoRA suffered from \QwenStructuredValidity{} JSON validity,
    all-direct relevance bias, and verbose target truncation. The primary
    benchmark split was changed from chronological to balanced
    duplicate-grouped 70/15/15, and Qwen training was reframed around compact
    labels rather than full gold JSON prose.
    \item[June 17, 2026, revalidation loop] The model-error audit was joined
    back to MongoDB labels and 40 high-priority records
    were flagged for source-grounded revalidation. The validation queue now
    filters \texttt{needsReview} records by audit priority, shows the model
    disagreement reason, and clears the flag only when the record is submitted,
    rejected, excluded, or adjudicated. This keeps dataset correction inside
    the existing annotation workflow rather than manual JSONL edits.
    \item[June 19, 2026] The 40 model-error revalidation records were reviewed
    and submitted through the MarketGyan validation workflow. All active
    revalidation flags are now cleared. The gold export was regenerated with
    500 records and the same target composition: 300 direct, 100 indirect, and
    100 not-relevant records. Placeholder text left by the targeted rebalance
    generator was repaired in 53 gold labels and 57 stored annotations. The
    refreshed \texttt{nepse-impact-500.jsonl} passed dataset validation, the
    500-record gate, and the balanced duplicate-grouped 350/75/75 split.
