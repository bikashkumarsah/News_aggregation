    schema-valid, evidence-grounded candidates. This exceeds the
    \CandidateCollectionTarget{}-candidate collection target. Duplicate

    and class-coverage gates, a frozen split manifest, finance-specific Qdrant
    indexing and retrieval, a protected CrewAI service boundary, local
    OpenAI-compatible Qwen inference configuration, grounded report APIs,
    idempotent post-market orchestration, and system-evaluation tooling.
    These components are implemented but have not produced training or live
    retrieval results because all \PendingCandidateCount{} candidates still
    require human decisions. OCR and legacy-font conversion are explicitly
    excluded from the project.
    \item[June 15, 2026] Human review exposed a corpus-quality problem: several
    schema-v1 approvals were general business stories without a defensible
    NEPSE impact mechanism. The v1 corpus was frozen as a pilot audit with
    \PilotApprovedCount{} approved, \PilotRejectedCount{} rejected,
    \PendingCandidateCount{} pending, and \QueuedCandidateCount{} queued

    adjudicated. A read-only selector found \VTwoDirectSelectionCount{} direct,
    \VTwoIndirectSelectionCount{} indirect, and
    \VTwoNegativeSelectionCount{} hard-negative candidates; therefore archive
    collection must continue into 2025 before the 350/100/100 corpus quota can
    be claimed.
    \item[June 15, 2026, 01:55 NPT] Targeted ShareSansar and resumable
    OnlineKhabar archive collection expanded the eligible pool to
    \EligibleDocumentCount{} documents. Exact-content deduplication removed
    \VTwoExactDuplicateCount{} duplicate candidates before selection. The
    resulting schema-v2 plan contains \VTwoDirectSelectionCount{} direct,
    \VTwoIndirectSelectionCount{} indirect, and
    \VTwoNegativeSelectionCount{} hard-negative records, with
    \VTwoEnglishSelectionCount{} English, \VTwoNepaliSelectionCount{} Nepali,
    \VTwoSymbolSelectionCount{} symbol-level records, at least 20 examples in
